Traditional Method

Fixed Collection Schedule

  • Trucks visit every bin regardless of fill level.
  • Excessive fuel consumption and vehicle wear.
  • Overflowing market bins remain unserviced between scheduled cycles.
  • Heavy carbon footprint and traffic congestion.
EcoRoute AI Solution

Predictive Selective Dispatch

  • Sensorless fill mathematical prediction engine.
  • Reroute trucks ONLY to bins at or exceeding 80% threshold.
  • Up to 45% reduction in total trip distance and diesel usage.
  • Integrated citizen verification and green rewards wallet.
